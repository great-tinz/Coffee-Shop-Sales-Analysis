# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (End-to-End)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes 6 months of transactional sales data (149,116 rows) for a coffee shop chain with three locations (Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, Lower Manhattan). The goal was to identify revenue drivers, track growth, and provide actionable recommendations for store management.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
*   **Excel:** Data cleaning, PivotTables, Dashboard creation.
*   **MySQL:** Database creation, data normalization (Star Schema), and SQL analysis.

## 📊 Key Insights
*   **Total Revenue:** $698,812.33 over the 6-month period.
*   **Growth:** Revenue doubled from January ($81k) to June ($166k).
*   **Top Store:** Hell's Kitchen slightly leads with $236,511.17.
*   **Top Product:** Coffee is the primary revenue driver ($269,952), followed by Tea ($196,405).
*   **Staffing:** There is a massive morning rush between 7 AM and 10 AM, with revenue dropping by half after 11 AM.

## 🚀 Recommendations
Management should schedule maximum staff between 7 AM and 10 AM to handle the rush and reduce wait times. Staffing can be reduced in the evening (after 6 PM) when sales drop significantly.
